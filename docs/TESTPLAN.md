# School Account Management System Test Plan

This test plan is derived from the specification in docs/SPECIFICATION.md and is intended to validate the documented business behavior of a compliant implementation. It does not rely on direct analysis of the legacy COBOL source.

## Scope

- Covers all documented business rules BR-01 through BR-06
- Covers all documented operations: View Balance, Credit Account, Debit Account, and Exit
- Covers menu navigation and session-scoped balance behavior

## Out of Scope

- Undefined or ambiguous behaviors called out for future domain review, including negative amounts, zero amounts, and non-numeric amount input handling
- Persistence beyond the documented in-memory session model
